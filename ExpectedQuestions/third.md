# Managerial Round (MR) - Expected Questions

## About Yourself

- Tell me about yourself.
- Walk me through your career journey.
- Why are you looking for a change?
- Why were you laid off?
- What are you looking for in your next role?

---

# Current Company & Project

- Tell me about your current company.
- Explain your role in VABRO.
- What are your daily responsibilities?
- How many team members are there?
- How do you coordinate with QA and Backend teams?
- What was your biggest achievement?

---

# Team Collaboration

- Describe a conflict with a teammate.
- How did you resolve it?
- Have you ever disagreed with a senior developer?
- How do you handle code review feedback?
- How do you help junior developers?

---

# Ownership Questions

- Tell me about a difficult task you owned.
- Describe a production issue you handled.
- Have you ever missed a deadline?
- How did you recover?
- How do you prioritize tasks?

---

# Agile Questions

- Explain Sprint Planning.
- How do you estimate story points?
- What happens during daily standups?
- What tools do you use for project tracking?
- How do you handle changing requirements?

---

# Problem Solving

- Describe a challenging bug.
- Describe a critical production issue.
- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
- What would you improve in your current project?

---

# Work Style

- Do you prefer working independently or in a team?
- How do you handle pressure?
- How do you manage multiple tasks?
- How do you learn new technologies?

---

# Career Questions

- Where do you see yourself in 3 years?
- What are your strengths?
- What are your areas for improvement?
- Why should we hire you?

---

# Company-Specific Questions

- Why do you want to join us?
- What do you know about our company?
- Why are you leaving your current company?
- What are your salary expectations?

---
